Decision-support interpretation

AHP provides the criterion weights used by the decision model. TOPSIS evaluates the alternatives using their weighted normalized criterion values and calculates relative closeness to the ideal and negative-ideal solutions. The resulting scores are analytical outputs of the specified model and should be interpreted together with the underlying GIS evidence.

CENTERLINE ANALYSIS

Intersection-Length Method

Length measurements are intentionally separated from footprint-based impact areas.

Impact Area

The permanent 3.5 m ground footprint is intersected with the relevant polygon layer. The resulting intersected area is converted from square metres to square kilometres.

Impact Area = Footprint ∩ Polygon

Intersection Length

The original HSR centerline is intersected with the relevant polygon layer. The resulting line length is converted from metres to kilometres.

Length = Centerline ∩ Polygon
Important distinction

The 3.5 m footprint is used for permanent ground-impact area calculations. It is not used to calculate the centerline intersection lengths.

INFRASTRUCTURE

Highway Interaction Method

Interaction Counting

Each identified highway/major-road interaction is counted as an individual infrastructure interaction for route comparison.

Road Classification

National Highways, State Highways and MDR are conceptually distinct classes. Where the supplied dataset does not separately identify MDR, it is reported as not separately identifiable rather than being inferred from another road class.

MCDM

AHP → TOPSIS Method

Multi-criteria decision-support workflow.

01

Criteria Weights

AHP pairwise comparisons are used to derive relative criterion weights.

02

Consistency Check

The consistency ratio is calculated to assess the consistency of the pairwise comparison matrix.

CR = 0.0133
03

TOPSIS Normalization

Criterion values are normalized and combined with the AHP-derived weights.

04

Ideal Solutions

Positive and negative ideal solutions are established for the weighted alternatives.

05

Relative Closeness

Each route receives a TOPSIS relative-closeness score for comparative analysis.

ROBUSTNESS

Sensitivity Analysis

The scenarios include baseline AHP, equal weights, environmental focus, agriculture focus, social development focus, infrastructure focus, water environment focus and forest focus.
